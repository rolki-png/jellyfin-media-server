# Prefer Direct-Playable audio over lossless remuxes

Plex on Xbox Series X plus this Samsung UE65U8092 cannot Direct Play TrueHD or DTS-HD (Plex’s Xbox MKV matrix omits them; the TV does not decode them on HDMI). We score those TRaSH custom formats −10000 and prefer DD+/AAC so *arr grab 4K WEB the Xbox can Direct Play instead of UHD remuxes that hitch on audio remux or subtitle burn-in.
